# Claims & Escalation Path (after the sale)

Load this file when a purchase **breaks, arrives wrong, or the warranty is refused** — or pre-emptively for any recommended high-value buy so the user knows the path *before* they need it. This is the post-purchase half of the loop: the warranty is part of what was paid for.

> [!important] The warranty is part of the deal
> A cheaper price on a product with an honored warranty beats a lower price on one with none. Keep the invoice, register the warranty on time, and know the escalation path before the product fails.

## 1. Prevention (do this at purchase time)

- Keep the **GST invoice** (and the bank/card statement that proves payment) — this is the single most important document for any claim
- **Register the warranty** within the deadline (many brands: 30 days from purchase or never)
- Save the serial number, model, seller, and order ID in the tracker row (see `tracker.md`)
- Check the warranty *terms* at purchase: what's covered, what's excluded (battery, accidental), and whether it's brand or refurbisher warranty

## 2. The claims ladder (lowest effort → highest)

1. **Brand service center** — contact the brand's official support/ASC with invoice + serial; most warranty claims resolve here
2. **Platform support** — if bought on a marketplace, the seller/platform mediates first (arrived-wrong, DOA, counterfeit, seller warranty)
3. **National Consumer Helpline (NCH)** — call **1915** or file at **consumerhelpline.gov.in**; a logged complaint often unlocks a response the brand ignored for weeks
4. **e-Daakhil** (online consumer forum) — free, online consumer-complaint filing at **edaakhil.nic.in**; refund/replacement/compensation can be awarded
5. **District consumer commission** — only for amounts above the e-Daakhil/forum limit, or if the brand ignores the forum order
6. Whatever the outcome, **log it** — a covered repair goes in the repair ledger as `Paid by = warranty` (see `repairs.md`); a denied claim becomes an OOP repair that tells the truth about durability

## 3. What to prepare before escalating

- Invoice, order screenshot, serial number, warranty-registration proof
- A timeline: when bought, when it failed, every support ticket/response (email trail)
- Photos/videos of the fault (especially if it's intermittent — record it while it's failing)
- The exact defect + what you're asking for (repair / replacement / refund)

## 4. Price protection (the other post-purchase claim)

- Platform price-drop windows (often 7 days) — check the order page for "price dropped"; not guaranteed
- Return-and-rebuy only if the drop exceeds the return/restocking friction
- Premium-card purchase protection — refund if the price drops within N days; cite the card's terms, don't assume (see `finance.md` section 8)

## 5. Boundaries

- This skill **prepares the evidence and gives the path** — it does not file the complaint for the user, call the helpline, or guarantee any outcome
- Warranty terms vary by brand and listing — always verify the *specific* product's terms before promising a repair/replacement
